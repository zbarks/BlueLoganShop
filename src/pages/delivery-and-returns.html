Delivery

We deliver to addresses in the United Kingdom. Most orders are packed and sent within five working days, and you will receive an email with a tracking number once your order is on its way.

Returns

If you are not happy with your order, please contact us within 14 days of receiving it. Items should be returned unused and in their original packaging. One-off and made-to-order pieces may not be returnable unless they arrive damaged.

Questions

Please use the contact form and we will reply by email.
